# LinkedIn post template

Copy this file to `outputs/post.md`, fill it in, delete the guidance notes,
and post the "Post text" section with your infographic attached.

Target length: **100 to 200 words**. LinkedIn truncates after about the
first three lines ("...see more"), so the disclosure and the hook both have
to live in those lines.

---

## Post text

<Line 1-2: the hook AND the course context together. Two shapes that work:>

> I'm taking DSAN 6725 (Applied Generative AI) at Georgetown this fall, and
> one idea from class keeps following me around: <the idea, in one clause>.

> In my Applied GenAI course at Georgetown (DSAN 6725) we measured something
> this week that changed how I think about <topic>: <the one-line finding>.

<Body, 2-3 short paragraphs or 3-4 plain bullets. One idea. A concrete
number or example from your own lab work beats a general claim: "our agent
passed 11 of 12 questions once, but only 8 of 12 three times in a row"
teaches more than "reliability matters". Spell terms out on first use.>

<Closing line: what you would tell a colleague to look at, or the one
question the topic leaves you with. A real question invites comments; a
summary of what you just said invites nothing.>

<Optional: 2-4 hashtags at the end, no more. #GenAI #DSAN6725 and one or
two specific to the topic.>
