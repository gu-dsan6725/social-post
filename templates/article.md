# Long-form template: LinkedIn or Medium article, plus the companion post

The long option: a 600 to 1,200 word article published on LinkedIn or
Medium, shared on LinkedIn with a short companion post. The companion post
carries the disclosure and the infographic; the article carries the depth.

Copy this file to `outputs/post.md` and fill in both halves.

## The article

**Title:** <plain and specific beats clever: "What pass^k taught me about
agent reliability" beats "The Hidden Truth About AI Agents">

**Outline:**

1. **The setup** (2-3 sentences): the course context in the first
   paragraph. You are learning this in DSAN 6725 at Georgetown; here is the
   question the class raised.
2. **The idea**, explained to a smart colleague who has not taken the
   course. Spell out every term on first use. One idea; resist the second.
3. **The worked piece**: a number you computed, a trace you read, a failure
   you caused and diagnosed in a lab. Your own artifact is what makes this
   article yours; screenshots of your own runs are welcome here.
4. **What it changes**: one or two paragraphs on what you now do
   differently, at the scale of your own work. No industry predictions.
5. **Pointers**: 2-3 links to primary sources (papers, docs, the repo you
   used).
