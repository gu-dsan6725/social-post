# Posts to critique

Four flawed posts for the critique round. Each one breaks the advice on
purpose, so they contain the em dashes, hype words and emoji that the rest
of this folder bans. Show the post first, let the room call out problems,
then reveal the list.

For each post, ask:

1. Would you click "...see more"?
2. What is the one idea?
3. What came from the writer's own work?
4. Which giveaways do you spot?
5. How would you rewrite the first line?

## 1. The AI voice

> 🚀 RAG is dead. Long context killed it.
>
> I just finished an incredible deep dive into Retrieval-Augmented
> Generation, and let me tell you: it's not about retrieval, it's about
> context.
>
> Here's what I learned:
> ✅ Chunking matters.
> ✅ Embeddings matter.
> ✅ Evaluation matters.
>
> The future of AI is here — and it's agentic. Are you ready to unleash its
> power? 💡
>
> In short: context is king. 👑
>
> #AI #GenAI #RAG #LLM #MachineLearning #Innovation #FutureOfWork #Tech

Problems:

- A rocket and an unbacked hot take in line 1
- No writer, no course, no context
- "deep dive", "unleash", "the future of AI is here"
- "It's not about X, it's about Y"
- Three ✅ fragments, then an em-dash reveal
- A recap closer and eight hashtags
- Zero numbers. What did they learn?

## 2. The textbook

> Transformers are a neural network architecture introduced in 2017 in the
> paper "Attention Is All You Need." They use a mechanism called
> self-attention, which allows each token to attend to every other token in
> the sequence. This is computed using queries, keys and values. Multi-head
> attention runs several attention operations in parallel. Positional
> encodings are added because attention by itself has no sense of order.
> Transformers now power models such as GPT and Claude, and they have also
> been applied to vision and audio.
>
> This semester I learned about this in my DSAN course at Georgetown.
>
> #AI

Problems:

- Lines 1 to 3 read like a dictionary entry
- The context comes last, after the cut
- Five ideas in one post
- Nothing from the writer's own work
- Passive voice: "is computed", "are added"
- Open with one attention step you worked by hand

## 4. The article opening

> **Understanding RAG: A Comprehensive Guide**
>
> In today's fast-paced world of artificial intelligence, Retrieval-Augmented
> Generation (RAG) has emerged as a transformative paradigm that is
> revolutionizing how organizations leverage their data. In this
> comprehensive guide, we will delve into the intricacies of RAG, exploring
> its key components, benefits and challenges. Whether you are a seasoned
> practitioner or a curious beginner, this article will equip you with
> everything you need to navigate this exciting landscape.

Problems:

- "In today's fast-paced world"
- paradigm, revolutionizing, leverage, delve, navigate, landscape
- Announces what it will say instead of saying it
- No particular reader in mind
- 67 words, no facts, no writer
- Start with the question your project answered
