# Clarksburg High School version

A 60-minute version of the workshop for juniors and seniors at Clarksburg
High School. It has no writing session and no draft review. Students see
how posts work, read three model posts, and pick apart three flawed posts
together.

## Run of show

| Time | Part | What happens |
|------|------|--------------|
| 0:00 to 0:25 | 1. How posts work | Age limits and privacy, one idea, the story spine, openings, formats, timing, the image, editing with AI, posting on a schedule |
| 0:25 to 0:35 | 2. Model posts | A LinkedIn post on an AP Statistics survey, an X thread on the birthday problem, a Medium article on a science fair model |
| 0:35 to 0:50 | 3. Spot the problems | Three flawed posts; the room calls out what's wrong before each answer appears |
| 0:50 to 1:00 | Questions | |

## Notes for this audience

- LinkedIn requires users to be 16 or older; X and Medium allow 13 and up,
  and X has a parent or guardian accept the terms for anyone under 18. Slide
  3 says so before anything else.
- The model posts name the class and the project but no school, location or
  schedule. Keep any live examples you add to the same rule.
- The numbers in the project stories are examples. The birthday math is
  exact.
