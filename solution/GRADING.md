# Grading Runbook: social-post

Instructor-only; withheld from student copies. 50 points. Student repos are
`<lab>-<netid>-fall-2026`; the live post URL in `outputs/post_url.txt` is
the primary artifact, and the committed text and image must match it.

## Mechanical pass

- `outputs/post.md`, `outputs/infographic.png` (or .jpg), and
  `outputs/post_url.txt` all present.
- The URL loads and is public. The live text matches the committed text
  apart from trivial edits.
- The image on the live post is the committed image.

## Rubric

| What | Points | Check |
|------|--------|-------|
| Disclosure up front | 10 | DSAN 6725 and Georgetown named in the first three lines (post) or first paragraph (article), before the content starts. Buried or missing disclosure caps the lab at half credit |
| The explanation | 15 | One topic from the course, technically correct, terms spelled out, and ideally anchored in the student's own lab artifact (a number, a trace, a failure). Generic topic summaries earn the floor, not the ceiling |
| The infographic | 10 | One idea, legible on a phone, matches the post, no garbled or misspelled text (AI-generated images are fine; unread AI text in them is not) |
| Reads like a person | 10 | None of the banned tells from the README: no hype words, no "not X but Y" shapes, no em-dash reveals, no fragment triplets, no recap closer, no unsupportable hot takes |
| Logistics | 5 | 100-200 words (or thread/article per template), posted or scheduled 9am Tue/Wed/Thu, checklist items done |

Liking or reposting classmates' posts carries **no points**: it is
desirable and voluntary, and the README says exactly that.

## Shortcuts to catch

- A post that is live but differs from the committed text: grade the live
  one, note the mismatch.
- The blank-AI-draft voice: flawless rhythm, zero specifics from their own
  labs, interchangeable with anyone else's post. The fix was in the README
  (write first, edit with AI after); grade "reads like a person"
  accordingly.
- A stock image or meme instead of an infographic: the image requirement
  is an infographic carrying the post's idea.
